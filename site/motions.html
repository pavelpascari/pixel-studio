Figures and motions

Figures are skeletons with motion models: feet planted with inverse kinematics, hips that rise and fall, heads that hold their gaze, tails that follow through. Characters dressed on a figure get every motion.

Tune a motion

A bare figure (no parts) is drawn as its skeleton. Change a motion's parameters and watch the feet: in a walk, a planted foot must not slide. Try "stride": 20 (too long: validation reports the foot that misses), or "lift": 8.

Every parameter is listed in docs/figures.md.
