## Measured from photographs

These motions come from Eadweard Muybridge's *Animal Locomotion* (1887, public domain, scans from the Boston Public Library) and published measurements. Every value, and every frame of the sit-down and stand-up, is logged with the plate frame or table it came from in [`references/`](https://github.com/pavelpascari/pixel-studio/blob/main/packages/pixel-studio/references/README.md) (raw measurements, one file per plate, in `references/measurements/`).

| motion | from | what the source sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dog.gallop` | plates 708–710 (racing hounds) | 0.25 s cycle, each paw down 22% of it, hind–hind–fore–fore footfalls, two flight phases |
| `human.sprint` | plates 60, 62, 64 (running full speed) | 0.5 s cycle, feet down 29%, elbows bent about 90°, big arm swing |
| `human.sitDown` | plate 236, frames 6–12 | hip height and trunk lean of every frame; 183 ms between frames |
| `human.standUp` | plate 249, frames 1–10 | lean forward to 34° with the hips still down, then rise; 92 ms between frames |
| `goose.fly` | Meir et al. 2019; plates 755, 765 (pigeon, vulture) | 5 wingbeats a second; the wing sweeps from high above the back to below the body, and comes up folded |

## Tune a motion

A bare figure (no `parts`) is drawn as its skeleton. Change a motion's parameters and watch the feet: in a walk, a planted foot must not slide. Try `"stride": 20` (too long: validation reports the foot that misses), or `"lift": 8`.
