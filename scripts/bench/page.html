A small page about a bakery

We bake bread every morning from six. Sourdough, rye and a seeded loaf are on the counter by seven; pastries follow at eight. Orders for the weekend close on Thursday evening.

The shop is on the corner of the market square, next to the bike repair. There is a bench outside and coffee inside.

Bread

Sourdough, rye, seeded.

Pastries

Croissants, buns, tarts.

Coffee

Filter and espresso.

Opening hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 6:00 to 14:00. Closed on Mondays.
