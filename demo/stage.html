Trail notes

Scroll down quickly: root the goose keeps you company (keep-in-view). If you get away, it runs to catch up, or drops in from the top of the screen when you are too far. Tap a character several times quickly and it panics.

Spring brings mud to the lower paths; take the ridge after rain. The lakeside loop is flat and good for an evening. Old forest is cool in summer, with long stretches of roots. Summit is short, steep and worth it at sunrise.

Pack water, a layer, and a snack for the goose (it will ask). Leave gates as you find them. Keep to the paths where the ground is soft. Dogs on leads near the lake, please.
